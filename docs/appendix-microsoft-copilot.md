# Appendix: Microsoft Copilot (general staff)

*Approved for general staff (AI policy, Appendix A). Use it on a work device, signed in with your work account (Section 5).*

## Open Copilot
- In a browser: go to **copilot.cloud.microsoft** *[confirm the address IT prefers]*, or
- In **Microsoft Teams** or **Outlook**: select **Copilot** in the left bar, or
- In **Word, Excel or PowerPoint**: select the **Copilot** button on the Home tab (if your license includes it).

## Before you paste anything
1. **Check your account:** your work email appears at the top right. If it shows a personal account, sign out and sign in with your work account.
2. **Look for the Protected label** (a shield icon) near the chat box. It means enterprise data protection is on for your work account. **No shield? Don't paste internal content.** Ask the Helpdesk.
3. **Personal information never goes in.** Internal documents may (rule R21).

## Good habits
- Start each new topic in a **new chat**.
- Plan with the [prompt-planning template](02-prompt-planning-template.md); copy prompts from the [prompt library](03-prompt-library.md).
- Ask for **one fix at a time**; read every word of the output.
- Copy the final text into Word or `content.js` yourself; put real details back outside Copilot.
- Don't use Copilot to decide anything about a learner or staff member (rule R12).

## What Copilot is good for in course building
| Good fit | Not a fit |
|---|---|
| Outlines, lesson text, questions and feedback, practice tasks, theme ideas, emails, checklists, alt text | Running, testing or packaging the course; editing many code files; deciding who passes |
