# AI rules for course builders (one page)

*For anyone at [ORGANIZATION] who uses AI to help build training. The rule numbers (R1, R2 …) match the [policy map](00-policy-map.md), which quotes the AI Acceptable Use Policy word for word.*

## Four rules to remember
1. **Use Microsoft Copilot, on a work device, signed in with your work email.** (R1, R4) Claude is approved only for technical staff and software developers (R2). Any other tool needs approval from the Chief Information Officer first (R3).
2. **Never paste anything internal or personal into any AI tool, Copilot included.** (R5, R6, R7) Give the AI a cleaned-up, made-up version instead.
3. **A person checks every output.** (R9) If you can't confirm something is correct, your manager approves it before it's used.
4. **Say when AI helped.** Published training includes a line such as "Created with help from AI tools and reviewed by [TEAM]." (R10)

## Safe to paste / never paste

| ✅ Safe to paste | ⛔ Never paste (R5–R8) |
|---|---|
| Your own made-up sample text and placeholders | **Trade secrets and internal know-how:** processes, pricing, enrollment or business strategy, financials |
| Public information (published websites, public laws) | **Student records** (protected by FERPA): names, IDs, grades, attendance, financial aid details |
| General questions ("How do I write a good quiz question?") | **Employee personal information:** names with job details, IDs, pay, HR matters |
| Requests for formats ("Make a 3-question quiz on [TOPIC]") | **Learner data from ADP Learning:** reports, scores, answers, completion lists |
| Wording fixes on text that already has placeholders | **Passwords, keys, links that need sign-in,** system screenshots |
| Generic examples ("a student asks about [POLICY TOPIC]") | **Unreleased content:** draft policies, internal memos, slide decks, not-yet-public training |

**Not sure?** Treat it as "never paste" and ask your manager.

## Also off-limits
- **No deepfakes:** no AI images or voices of real people, and no copying protected images, music or text. (R11)
- **No AI decisions about people:** AI may not grade, rank or decide anything about learners or staff. (R12)
- **No workarounds:** never get around security or sign-in controls, and never buy an AI tool without approval. (R15, R17)
- **Code gets checked:** AI-written code is reviewed and tested before anyone uses it. (R13)

## Clean-up checklist (before you paste anything)
- [ ] People's names → `[LEARNER]`, `[MANAGER]`, `[INSTRUCTOR]`
- [ ] Organization, campus and program names → `[ORGANIZATION]`, `[CAMPUS]`, `[PROGRAM NAME]`
- [ ] Numbers (dollars, dates, counts, IDs, scores) → `[AMOUNT]`, `[DATE]`, `[NUMBER]`
- [ ] Internal steps or specifics → a general description ("a 3-step approval process")
- [ ] Links, emails, phone numbers → `[POLICY LINK]`, `[SUPPORT EMAIL]`, `[PHONE]`
- [ ] Read it once more: could an outsider work out who or what this is about? If yes, clean it up further.
- [ ] Afterward: put the real details back yourself, **outside** the AI tool.

*Questions or a suspected violation? Contact the Chief Information Officer (R18). Your AI use on work systems may be monitored (R16).*
