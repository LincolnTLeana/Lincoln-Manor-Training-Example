# AI rules for course builders (one page)

*For anyone at [ORGANIZATION] who uses AI to help build training. The rule numbers (R1, R2 …) match the [policy map](00-policy-map.md), which quotes the AI Acceptable Use Policy word for word.*

## Four rules to remember
1. **Use Microsoft Copilot, on a work device, signed in with your work email.** (R1, R4) Look for the **Protected** label (shield icon) before you paste anything. Other tools need approval from the Chief Information Officer first (R3).
2. **Internal documents may go into Copilot, and only Copilot.** (R21) **Personal information never goes into any AI tool.** (R6, R8)
3. **A person checks every output.** (R9) If you can't confirm something is correct, your manager approves it before it's used.
4. **Say when AI helped.** Published training includes a line such as "Created with help from AI tools and reviewed by [TEAM]." (R10)

## OK in Copilot / never paste

| ✅ OK in Copilot (work sign-in, Protected label) | ⛔ Never paste, in any AI tool (R6, R8) |
|---|---|
| Internal training materials, job aids, process steps | **Student records** (protected by FERPA): names, IDs, grades, attendance, financial aid details |
| Internal policies and procedures you are allowed to see | **Employee personal information:** names with job details, IDs, pay, HR matters |
| Drafts of courses, emails and announcements | **Learner data from ADP Learning:** reports, scores, answers, completion lists |
| Program and campus names | **Passwords, keys, access codes** and screenshots of systems showing personal data |
| Public information and general questions | **Anything about a specific student's situation** (R7) |

**Not sure?** Leave it out and ask your manager. **Internal documents go into Copilot only:** never into another AI tool, a personal account or a personal device.

## Also off-limits
- **No deepfakes:** no AI images or voices of real people, and no copying protected images, music or text. (R11)
- **No AI decisions about people:** AI may not grade, rank or decide anything about learners or staff. (R12)
- **No workarounds:** never get around security or sign-in controls, and never buy an AI tool without approval. (R15, R17)
- **Code gets checked:** AI-written code is reviewed and tested before anyone uses it. (R13)

## Before you paste: remove personal information
- [ ] People's names → `[LEARNER]`, `[MANAGER]`, `[INSTRUCTOR]`
- [ ] Student and employee IDs, dates of birth, contact details → `[ID]`, `[DATE]`, `[EMAIL]`, `[PHONE]`
- [ ] Real cases and stories about a person → a made-up example ("a student misses a deadline")
- [ ] Learner results or reports → leave them out
- [ ] Read it once more: could anyone tell *who* this is about? If yes, keep removing details.
- [ ] Afterward: put real names or details back yourself, **outside** the AI tool.

*Questions or a suspected violation? Contact the Chief Information Officer (R18). Your AI use on work systems may be monitored (R16).*
