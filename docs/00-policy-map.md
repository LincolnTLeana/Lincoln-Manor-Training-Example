## Open questions for the Chief Information Officer (not decided by this toolkit)

1. **Section 4 vs. Copilot.** Section 4 bans proprietary information in *any* "GenAI tool chatbot." Section 6 limits the ban to "public or non-secure" systems. Until the CIO answers in writing, the toolkit follows the stricter reading: **no proprietary information in Copilot either.**
2. **Who counts as "Technical Staff"?** Appendix A doesn't define it. Does the help desk count? The IT director?
3. **Does "Claude" in Appendix A include Claude Code** (the coding version of the tool)?
4. **Is generating training images, audio or music with AI an "approved purpose"?** If so, under what license terms?
