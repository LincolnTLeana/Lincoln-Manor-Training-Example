# Policy map: where each toolkit rule comes from

**Source:** [the organization] IT Policies Manual, *AI Acceptable Use Policy (AI AUP)*, effective January 30, 2026 (last revised and reviewed January 30, 2026).
**Read the current version** on the intranet. A copy is kept in this repository: [reference/AI_Acceptable_Use_Policy.pdf](../reference/AI_Acceptable_Use_Policy.pdf). Section 11 says each person is "responsible for staying informed about the latest version of the policy." If the policy changes, update this map first, then the documents that cite it.

Quotes are the policy's own words. Square brackets mark the only edits: [the organization] replaces the organization's name, and the Chief Information Officer is named by role only.

## Open questions for the Chief Information Officer (not decided by this toolkit)

1. **Section 4 vs. Copilot (R21).** Section 4 bans proprietary information in *any* "GenAI tool chatbot." Section 6 limits the ban to "public or non-secure" systems "without proper authorization." The toolkit currently allows internal documents in Copilot as an interim project decision. **Next step:** a Section 5 request to the CIO (tool: Microsoft Copilot; purpose: building staff training; data: internal training material, no personal information) to get that authorization in writing.
2. **Who counts as "Technical Staff"?** Appendix A doesn't define it. Does the help desk count? The IT director?
3. **Does "Claude" in Appendix A include Claude Code** (the coding version of the tool)?
4. **Is generating training images, audio or music with AI an "approved purpose"?** If so, under what license terms?
